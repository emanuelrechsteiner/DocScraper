## FAQ

### Can I try Parsify for free?
Yes! The Free tier requires no credit card and gives you 100 requests per hour to evaluate the service.

### What happens if I exceed my limits?
At 80% usage you'll receive a warning. At 120% (soft limit) requests continue with a warning header. At 150% (hard limit) requests are blocked until the next billing period.

### Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period.

### Do you offer annual billing?
Not yet, but it's on our roadmap. Contact us for custom enterprise pricing.
